# **ORANGE KITE STUDIO**

## **Brand Identity Design Guide**

**Version 1.0 \| June 2026 Brand Direction**

**Positioning:** Creative intelligence for market leaders.  
**Primary Tagline:**Rise with intention. Lead with impact.  
**Brand Philosophy:** Bring our Alpha game to turn our clients into the Alpha of their market.

---

# **1\. THE CREATIVE BRIEF**

## **The Objective**

Develop a distinctive visual identity for Orange Kite Studio, a full-service creative agency based in Lahore, Pakistan.

The identity must communicate strategic intelligence, competitive ambition, creative excellence, and calm confidence. It should feel premium and contemporary without becoming cold, corporate, or unnecessarily aggressive.

The brand should be memorable because of its design quality and thinking—not because it relies on predictable symbols of dominance.

## **The Desired Impression**

When someone encounters Orange Kite Studio, the brand should communicate:

* **Intelligent:** We think before we create.  
* **Ambitious:** We want our clients to compete at a higher level.  
* **Distinctive:** We bring a clear point of view.  
* **Refined:** Every design decision has a purpose.  
* **Playful:** We enjoy ideas, experimentation, and the creative process.  
* **Grounded:** We stay composed, honest, and dependable under pressure.

## **The Central Design Idea**

**A kite with an edge.**

The kite represents movement, direction, ambition, and the ability to rise. The orange connects the identity to the founders' roots in Sargodha.

The result should be a sophisticated creative mark—not a literal illustration of a fruit or a festival kite.

# **2\. LOGO DESIGN SYSTEM**

## **Primary Concept: The Orange-Kite Fusion**

Develop a distinctive symbol that merges the silhouette or geometry of a kite with the structure of an orange.

### **Creative Routes to Explore**

**Route A — The Geometric Fusion**

A clean, angular kite silhouette with subtle internal geometry inspired by orange segments.

Desired feel: modern, intelligent, minimal, recognisable.

**Route B — The Hidden Orange**

A kite whose central shape or negative space subtly reveals an orange.

Desired feel: clever, ownable, sophisticated.

**Route C — The Dynamic Mark**

A more directional kite symbol, with a carefully integrated orange-inspired centre.

Desired feel: energetic, ambitious, contemporary.

These are alternative design routes to explore, not three approved final logos.

## **Logo Requirements**

The final logo must:

* Remain legible at small sizes.  
* Work in full colour and monochrome.  
* Be recognisable without the wordmark.  
* Work against both light and dark backgrounds.  
* Avoid unnecessary detail and fragile lines.  
* Feel equally appropriate on a proposal, website, social profile, and client presentation.

## **Wordmark**

The full wordmark should read:

**ORANGE KITE STUDIO**

Typography should feel confident and refined. Explore a custom typographic treatment rather than simply typing the name in a standard font.

Test both uppercase and title-case versions before making a final decision.

## **Logo Lockups**

Create the following variations:

1. Primary horizontal logo: symbol plus full wordmark.  
2. Stacked logo: symbol above the wordmark.  
3. Standalone symbol: for profile images and favicons.  
4. Monochrome logo: single-colour application.  
5. Reversed logo: white or light mark on a dark background.

## **Avoid**

* Cartoon oranges or smiling fruit.  
* Overly literal kite illustrations.  
* Generic crowns, shields, and lightning bolts.  
* Gaming or esports aesthetics.  
* Excessive gradients and 3D effects.  
* Complicated linework that disappears at small sizes.

# **3\. COLOUR SYSTEM**

## **Primary Palette**

| Colour | Hex | Intended Use |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Signature Orange | \#E67E22 | Brand accents, highlights, calls to action |
| Deep Teal | \#0F766E | Supporting colour, selected backgrounds, secondary graphics |
| Sand Beige | \#E8DCCB | Editorial backgrounds and warm neutral surfaces |
| Charcoal | \#2C2C2C | Typography, dark layouts, high-contrast applications |
| White | \#FFFFFF | Negative space, clean layouts, readability |

## **Colour Strategy**

Orange should provide recognition. It does not need to dominate every composition.

Charcoal and white should establish clarity and sophistication. Teal should add depth, while sand beige should soften the overall system.

The identity should remain recognisable even when orange is used sparingly.

## **Recommended Starting Distribution**

For an initial digital design system, test approximately:

* 55% white or light neutral space.  
* 25% charcoal and dark neutral elements.  
* 10% signature orange.  
* 10% teal and beige accents.

These percentages are a starting point for exploration, not a rigid rule.

## **Colour Usage Rules**

* Use orange to direct attention to important elements.  
* Use charcoal for strong headlines and readable body text.  
* Use teal selectively to create contrast.  
* Use beige to add warmth without making the identity feel vintage.  
* Check contrast and readability before publishing.  
* Avoid using all colours at equal intensity in the same composition.

# **4\. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM**

## **Display Typeface: Playfair Display**

**Role:** Character and expression.

Use for:

* Selected headlines.  
* Editorial statements.  
* Website hero copy.  
* Campaign titles.  
* Brand manifesto excerpts.

Playfair Display provides an elegant editorial contrast to the more direct and competitive brand positioning.

Use it deliberately. Too much serif typography could make the agency feel like a luxury magazine or traditional consultancy.

## **Supporting Typeface: Inter**

**Role:** Clarity and performance.

Use for:

* Body copy.  
* Navigation.  
* Service descriptions.  
* Presentations.  
* Proposals.  
* Captions and digital interfaces.

Inter supports a contemporary, highly readable design system.

## **Typography Hierarchy**

**Display headline:** Playfair Display, with concise and memorable wording.

**Section heading:** Inter SemiBold or Bold.

**Body copy:** Inter Regular.

**Labels and metadata:** Inter Medium, with restrained use of uppercase.

## **Typography Principles**

* Prefer short, confident headlines.  
* Maintain generous spacing and clear hierarchy.  
* Avoid decorative fonts and excessive text effects.  
* Keep paragraph widths comfortable for reading.  
* Use consistent styles across documents and digital platforms.

The final designer should verify font availability and licensing for the intended use.

# **5\. GRAPHIC LANGUAGE**

## **Core Visual Motifs**

Build a supporting graphic system around the geometry and movement of the kite.

Potential elements include:

* Angular geometric forms.  
* Directional lines and controlled movement.  
* Cropped shapes inspired by the logo.  
* Strong grids and editorial compositions.  
* Strategic use of negative space.  
* Small orange accents that guide the viewer's attention.

These elements should support the logo rather than compete with it.

## **The Design Personality**

The overall look should combine:

**Precision \+ Movement \+ Restraint**

Precision comes from clear layouts and thoughtful typography.

Movement comes from directional forms, image composition, and controlled use of asymmetry.

Restraint comes from negative space, limited colours, and a refusal to decorate without purpose.

## **Graphic Elements to Avoid**

* Generic stock icons.  
* Random abstract shapes with no relationship to the brand.  
* Excessive patterns.  
* Overused motivational graphics.  
* Decorative cultural motifs added merely to make the design look Pakistani.  
* Aggressive visual effects associated with gaming brands.

# **6\. PHOTOGRAPHY AND ART DIRECTION**

## **Core Direction**

Photography should feel authentic, contemporary, cinematic, and intentional.

The visual identity should show people creating, thinking, building businesses, developing ideas, and interacting with the world around them.

## **Photography Categories**

### **Creative Process**

Show designers, strategists, photographers, filmmakers, and creative teams at work.

### **Business and Ambition**

Show founders, entrepreneurs, products, workplaces, and the real environments in which businesses operate.

### **Urban and Architectural Details**

Use Lahore and Punjab's streets, architecture, textures, and creative spaces when they add visual or narrative value.

### **Editorial Portraiture**

Use confident, natural portraits with thoughtful lighting and composition.

### **Campaign Imagery**

Develop art-directed concepts that express the client's message instead of relying on generic stock photography.

## **Image Treatment**

* Natural or carefully controlled lighting.  
* Strong composition.  
* Authentic expressions and environments.  
* Restrained colour grading.  
* Clear subjects and purposeful framing.  
* Minimal overlays and effects.

The agency's cultural background should appear organically in selected imagery. It should not dictate every visual or limit the agency's relevance to culturally focused clients.

# **7\. BRAND APPLICATIONS**

The identity should be tested in real situations before approval.

## **Website**

Design a clean, confident website with a strong opening statement, clear service architecture, selected case studies, and direct calls to action.

Suggested hero:

**Rise with intention. Lead with impact.**

Supporting statement:

“We combine strategy, branding, advertising, and creative execution to help ambitious businesses stand out and compete smarter.”

Primary call to action: **Let's Find Your Edge.**

## **Social Media**

Develop templates for:

* Case studies.  
* Creative insights.  
* Brand strategy tips.  
* Project announcements.  
* Team and collaborator recognition.  
* Campaign launches.  
* Agency perspectives and opinions.

Templates should be recognisable but flexible enough to accommodate different content formats.

## **Proposals and Presentations**

Use white or charcoal backgrounds, a restrained orange accent, clear typography, and strong editorial hierarchy.

The document should feel like the work of a strategic creative partner rather than a generic corporate template.

## **Business Stationery**

Develop business cards, letterheads, email signatures, invoices, and proposal covers using the same spacing, typography, and logo rules.

## **Video and Motion**

Explore a short logo animation inspired by lift, direction, and controlled movement.

Motion should feel deliberate and polished rather than explosive or overly dramatic.

# **8\. BRAND VOICE IN VISUAL COMMUNICATION**

The visual identity and the written voice must work together.

The writing is enthusiastic, casual, mostly respectful, and grounded by stoicism. The design should reflect that combination.

### **Example 1**

Headline: **Make Your Next Move Count.**

Visual treatment: A restrained geometric composition with a strong headline and a single orange accent.

### **Example 2**

Headline: **Good Ideas Need Direction.**

Visual treatment: A clear grid with directional graphic elements inspired by the kite.

### **Example 3**

Headline: **Stay Curious. Keep Your Head. Do the Work.**

Visual treatment: Minimal typography, generous negative space, and a confident editorial layout.

The goal is not to shout about being Alpha. It is to communicate competence, ambition, and thoughtful execution.

# **10\. SUCCESS CRITERIA**

The identity is ready for approval when it meets these criteria:

* It is recognisable without relying on the full brand name.  
* It communicates sophistication and competitive ambition.  
* The orange-kite concept is distinctive rather than generic.  
* It works in monochrome and at small sizes.  
* It remains clear across print and digital applications.  
* It does not depend on cultural imagery to communicate the brand.  
* It avoids clichéd alpha-male and gaming aesthetics.  
* It can accommodate a broad range of clients and industries.  
* It is supported by a consistent, usable design system.

# **FINAL CREATIVE DIRECTION**

**Orange Kite Studio is a refined, ambitious creative agency with a competitive mindset and a human core.**

Its identity should balance the energy of orange, the sophistication of deep teal and neutral tones, the editorial character of Playfair Display, and the clarity of Inter.

The kite provides the visual idea. The orange connects it to the founders' story. The strategy gives the brand its purpose.

The identity should feel capable of working with a small ambitious startup today and a major established brand tomorrow.

**Rise with intention. Lead with impact.**

*Orange Kite Studio — Creative intelligence for market leaders.*
